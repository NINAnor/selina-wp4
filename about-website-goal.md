# What is the goal of this website?
The primary goal of this website is to provide you with all the support you need to understand the underlying concepts of ecosystem service assessments and write a call for tender tailored to your needs! To this end we created three templates directly supporting the writing of a <b>Terms of Reference</b> underlying a call for tender. This website does not provide exhaustive support in writing an entire Terms of Reference but focuses on the three sections that are most likely to be challenging:

-	the <b>Frame & Scope</b> section aimed at describing the purpose(s) and reason(s) behind the need for commissioning an ecosystem service assessment; 

-	the <b>Methodology</b> section focused on detailing the technical characteristics and overall methodological process of the ecosystem service assessment; 

-	the <b>Evaluation Criteria</b> section whose goal is to precise the criteria according to which an ecosystem service assessment will be assessed by a commissioner. 

In addition to the support available in writing these sections, this website also provides more general information about general basic concepts on ecosystem services and the commissioning process.
